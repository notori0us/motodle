About Motodle

Motodle is a daily motorbike-guessing game. Guess the make, model and year of a real motorbike in five tries, from a photo that reveals a little more with every guess. A new one is posted every day.

Privacy

Motodle has no accounts, no cookies, no third-party trackers and no third-party scripts. Your guesses, statistics and settings are stored only in your own browser. The site keeps no access log of the requests your browser makes for it, and no identifier of any kind is sent or stored.
